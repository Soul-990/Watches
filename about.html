About Us

The story behind every second

Our Story

Basel & Co started in 1999 as a tiny repair bench with a simple belief: a watch should be more than a gadget, it should be a companion. Over twenty-five years we grew from fixing watches to designing them.

Today our collections blend classic design with durable, modern materials. We keep prices fair by selling directly to you, and we stand behind every watch with a two-year warranty.
